    The wing is sized to an 11.75 m/s design stall, % under R11's 12 m/s. Accept this margin and confirm stall in flight?
    The sizing reads the aircraft's trimmed CL_max from the aerodynamic package (now ) and designs to 11.75 m/s, so a CL_max up to 4 % below the prediction, or about 0.28 kg of mass growth, still meets R11. The margin cost 88 g and 0.6 min of cruise, no money and no pack change (docs/research/stall-margin.md compares 11.25–12.0 m/s). Transition completes at 16 m/s, 1.36 × the design stall.
    Recommended: accept, and measure stall in fixed-wing mode early in the flight build-up (test plan, stall test). Then set AIRSPEED_STALL to the measured value and AIRSPEED_MIN to the larger of 16 and 1.3 × the measured stall; above 13.08 m/s treat it as an R11 failure.

    R14; analysis/results.json · §3

    How do the stopped lift props sit in cruise?
    BLHeli_S ESCs cannot stop a prop at a set angle, so a 2-blade prop stops wherever it lands. The aerodynamic build-up puts the four stopped motors, mounts and props at a fifth of the aircraft's zero-lift drag, using estimated coefficients. The trade study shows C0 keeps R10 even at twice that drag, but only with the larger 8000 mAh pack, which costs most of the crash reserve.
    Recommended: run the car-roof test (§3 M1) on one stopped unit at 20 m/s. If the prop windmills or sits crosswise, print a stop pawl. Alternative: Eolo folding 17×6.2, $49.99 a pair. Two pairs cost $99.98, about $110 with tax, and that exceeds the reserve.

    docs/research/budget-lift.md caveat 4; Props table (folding) · §3

    MTOW lands at kg, under the 8–15 kg working target.
    The 8–15 kg range was a working target, not a hard limit; the hard cap is 25 kg. The cost cap and the short mission drive mass down. A smaller aircraft is cheaper, easier to carry (R5) and safer to test.
    Recommended: accept, and record it in the design basis as pending team sign-off. Alternative: add payload or ballast to reach 8 kg. That needs more battery, motor and money and buys nothing.

      Terms used in this review

      MTOW
      Maximum take-off mass, with payload and battery.
      T/W
      Thrust-to-weight ratio of the lift motors at full throttle.
      FM·η
      Figure of merit times motor and ESC efficiency: how well electrical power becomes hover thrust.
      CdA, CD0
      Drag area of a part (m²), and the aircraft's drag coefficient at zero lift.
      CL_max, stall
      Highest lift coefficient the wing reaches; it sets the slowest speed the wing can fly.
      MAC, static margin
      Mean aerodynamic chord; the distance from the centre of gravity back to the neutral point, as a share of it. Positive means stable in pitch.
      QuadPlane, Q modes
      ArduPilot's lift + cruise vehicle type and its hover modes (QHOVER, QLOITER, QLAND, QRTL).
      SITL
      Software in the loop: the real autopilot code flying a simulated aircraft on a computer.
      AGL
      Height above ground level.
      § 44809
      The US law covering recreational and educational drone flying (49 U.S.C. § 44809).

      The SOLIDWORKS model now matches the aircraft down to the screw: — part files in a —-component assembly, including every screw, nut, washer and heat-set insert at its real size. cad/build.ps1 regenerates it from the sizing outputs and cad/hardware.json, so the CAD can't drift from the numbers. Holes, counterbores and nut pockets are cut at the clearance and insert sizes in the hardware spec, with printed-hole compensation, and SOLIDWORKS interference detection has been run on the full assembly.

      • printed teardrop fairings over the four landing legs
      • servo trays, horns with hardpoints, pushrods, flight-controller and battery trays, pitot clamp, pusher cup

      params.mjs checks the pusher-to-lift-disc clearance, currently mm (minimum 20 mm). Interference detection reported 286 contacts: 201 are the intended press fits of the heat-set inserts, and the remaining 85 are slivers totalling 0.42 cm³, mostly where flat printed faces meet curved foam; the largest, 0.10 cm³, is the rudder clevis around its horn upright. It found two real clashes, both errors in the hardware spec, now corrected: the landing-leg screw must be M3 × 30 so the nut and head clear the tube, and the motor counterbore must be Ø7.5 to take the Ø7.0 washer.

        Joints and materials

        0 Controls and flight software

        The flight software is written, checked and rehearsed: ArduPlane 4.7.2 stable QuadPlane on the CoreWing F405 Wing APP V2, with a 129-parameter file that passes the checker and seven simulated failure scenarios.
