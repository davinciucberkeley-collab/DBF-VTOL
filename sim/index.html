Fly a lift + cruise eVTOL

Eight rotors lift you straight up. A pusher prop then speeds you up until the wing carries the weight, and the rotors stop and lock.

  1. Power on. Press P.Tap PWR.
  2. Auto takeoff to a 100 ft hover. Use Auto takeoff in the panel.Tap T/O.
  3. Throttle to 100%. Hold ↑.Drag the THR slider up. Watch the Lift bar move from rotors (amber) to wing (cyan).
  4. Auto land. It slows down, restarts the rotors and descends vertically.

Hand-flying: R/F climb and descend, W/S forward and back, A/D yaw, Q/E slide. Esc pauses.
