Landing gear: wheels (proposal)

Wheels cost {{GEAR_GOODS}} and raise the ground plane {{GEAR_DROP_MM}} mm EST; pusher disc clearance goes from {{GEAR_PUSH_BEFORE}} to {{GEAR_PUSH_AFTER}} mm EST. The legs keep their length. The wheels are not in the SOLIDWORKS model; the 3D view (§2) draws them with the Wheels (proposed) button.

docs/research/electrical-architecture.md §11; inventory/inventory.json rows G-01 to G-03; inventory/electrical/proposed_lines.json

Adopt wheeled gear?
Wheels lift the pusher disc off the ground by {{GEAR_PUSH_AFTER}} mm instead of {{GEAR_PUSH_BEFORE}} mm and keep the leg length, for {{GEAR_GOODS}} goods. They raise the CG from {{GEAR_CG0}} to {{GEAR_CG}} and cut the tip-back angle from {{GEAR_TIPBACK0}} to {{GEAR_TIPBACK}}. The reserve falls to {{PROP_AFTER}} with the capacitors ({{PROP_AFTER_NWA}} if the NWA props are free), against the {{TARGET}} target.
Recommended: adopt the 300SL wheels, re-quote the price, and fit-check the axle against the leg tip before ordering. Order the capacitors (G-04) either way: the FC and each ESC need one.
Alternative: keep the printed feet: $0, but the pusher disc stays {{GEAR_PUSH_BEFORE}} mm from the ground. Treaded wheels (DUB300T, {{GEAR_ALT1}} g each, ${{GEAR_ALT1P}}) are the research file's other option.

What the team must measure (M1 to M5)
  1. M1
    Prop mass match. Weigh every received prop on one scale. Outliers beyond the designer's tolerance (—) are rejected.
  2. M2
    Static and dynamic balance. Static on a low-friction balancer, then dynamic on a stand with vibration and RPM sensors. A dynamic result above G 6.3 fails.
  3. M3
    Thrust-stand comparison vs the JeeFly and APC baseline. Same motor, ESC and battery; compare thrust, current and efficiency at the hover point of {{HOVER_N}} N per rotor and at max throttle. Pass or fail threshold: — (the sources give none). Stay inside 26 A for 30 s.
  4. M4
    Max-RPM spin-up behind a shield. Step up in RPM and look for vibration, blade flutter or deformation. No-load reference about 10,000 RPM EST. Threshold: —.
  5. M5
    Tip clearance. Measure the gap between each prop disc and the airframe and boom. Numeric limit: —.

docs/research/nautical-wings-props.md §3–4; hover thrust 6.18 kg / 4 = 1.545 kg = {{HOVER_N}} N as shown there
