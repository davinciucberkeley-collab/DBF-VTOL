1 Overview

The Inventory Czar owns every physical thing between a purchase decision and a flight. That is buying, receiving, stock, the parts the team makes from raw stock, one kit bin per subassembly (K1 to K7), and the carbon propellers Nautical Wings Aerospace (NWA) designs on demand, which the team reports it can get free. Verdict: the plan fits the cap, with a thin margin. Committed spend is {{COMMITTED}} of the {{CAP}} cap, which leaves a {{RESERVE}} crash reserve against a target of {{TARGET}}. As of {{DATE}} the master list has {{LINES}} lines ({{N_BUY}} to buy, {{N_MAKE}} to make, {{N_NWA}} NWA prop lines) and {{RECEIVED}} are received. Two items block or erode the plan: the NWA terms, which are not confirmed, and the {{SHIP}} shipping allowance, which is an estimate.

Open decisions

Confirm the Nautical Wings prop terms in writing
Nothing public confirms that NWA offers free or on-demand props to students: the company is a Bengaluru electric-motor maker, and the only prop range found is a 14 to 40 in standard series, cited in a Tyto Robotics customer story. The inventory proposes {{NWA_QTY}} NWA props ({{NWA_LIFT}} lift, {{NWA_PUSH}} pusher) that replace the retail JeeFly 1755 and APC 14x8.5EP lines, and those retail lines are on hold until it is settled. Blocking for prop ordering.
Recommended: email NWA the spec sheet (§6) and ask for price, lead time, shipping and import cost, and balance report in writing. Keep the retail lines on hold, not deleted, until the reply arrives.
Alternative: release the on-hold retail lines (JeeFly 1755 and APC 14x8.5EP, {{HOLD_GOODS}} goods) now and treat NWA props as an optional upgrade. Cost: reserve stays at {{RESERVE}}, not {{RES_IF}} ({{PRICES_CHECKED}} prices).

Who NWA is

  • Nautical Wings Aerospace, Bengaluru, India: the most likely match, and the only company found under this exact name. It was founded in 2020 and sells B2B hardware to aircraft makers.

  • Props: a Tyto customer page lists “standard series propellers from 14 to 40 inches” and a 30 in fixed prop. Carbon layup, hub and bore, balancing, thrust data, lead time, ordering process and prices: — (not in any source found).

Acceptance tests on receipt

docs/research/nautical-wings-props.md §4 (UST balancing article, ArduPilot vibration page, Tyto balancing guide, GetFPV motor page, as cited there). The checkboxes are not saved; record results in the sheet.

What the team must measure

  1. M1
    Prop mass match. Weigh every received prop on one scale. Outliers beyond the designer's tolerance (—) are rejected.
  2. M2
    Static and dynamic balance. Static on a low-friction balancer, then dynamic on a stand with vibration and RPM sensors. A dynamic result above G 6.3 fails.
  3. M3
    Thrust-stand comparison vs the JeeFly and APC baseline. Same motor, ESC and battery; compare thrust, current and efficiency at the hover point of {{HOVER_N}} N per rotor and at max throttle. Pass or fail threshold: — (the sources give none). Stay inside 26 A for 30 s.
  4. M4
    Max-RPM spin-up behind a shield. Step up in RPM and look for vibration, blade flutter or deformation. No-load reference about 10,000 RPM EST. Threshold: —.
  5. M5
    Tip clearance. Measure the gap between each prop disc and the airframe and boom. Numeric limit: —.

LiPo handling and storage

  • Packs: the Ovonic 6S 6500 mAh 2-pack (P-12) is the flight battery. Note: {{P12_NOTE}}.
  • Storage: a LiPo bag (P-18, Tenergy B08QCMMGJT, 7 × 9 in; one 169 × 49 × 63 mm pack fits EST) inside the Harbor Freight 0.50 cal ammo can (P-26; inside 283 × 140 × 175 mm). Two 49 mm packs side by side are 98 mm, under the 140 mm width.
  • Ammo-can caution: a gasketed can is meant to seal. Teams commonly remove or slit the gasket so a venting cell cannot pressurise the can. That is general practice, not from the Harbor Freight page; confirm it against the university EH&S LiPo guidance (—).
  • Charging: the HTRC C150 (P-13) gives 150 W / 25.2 V = {{C150_A}} A on 6S. One 6.5 Ah pack takes 6.5 / {{C150_A}} = {{PACK_H}} h before the constant-voltage taper EST.
  • Storage-charge level: — (not in the repo sources; read it from the pack maker's sheet before the first storage). Do not leave packs charged or unattended on the charger.
